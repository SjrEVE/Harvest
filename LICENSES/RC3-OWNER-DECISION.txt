RC3 LICENSE REVIEW STATUS
=========================
Available license texts are bundled under LICENSES/runtime and LICENSES/qt.
Qt texts are from the official qt/qtbase v6.12.0 tag; SHA256 and source URLs
are recorded in LICENSES/qt/SOURCES.json. Runtime dependency licenses were
copied from the installed locked wheels, including pure Python modules
compiled into the executables, and CPython LICENSE.txt.
